About Peppermeds

Reliable coordination for healthcare supply needs.

PEPPERMEDS BIOTECH LLP is a Pune-based pharmaceutical wholesale, trading and distribution business focused on procurement inquiries, specialized healthcare products, surgical consumables and supply coordination for professional customers.

Established in March 2025, we aim to build long-term business relationships through responsive communication, clear quotations and careful coordination of institutional requirements.

  • Based in Hadapsar, Pune, Maharashtra
  • Focused on B2B and institutional inquiries
  • Product and supply terms confirmed case by case
  • Compliance information shared only when verified
